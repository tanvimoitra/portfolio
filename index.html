FAQs

Questions people ask before hiring

What is a marketing freelancer?

An independent marketer you hire for specific work or a set period, instead of taking on an employee. I work with B2B SaaS and B2C teams on content, product marketing, growth strategy and sales enablement, scoped as a project, a monthly retainer or hourly support.

Is AI replacing freelancers?

AI speeds up drafting and research, but it doesn't know your buyers, where your product is hardest to sell or what your sales team hears on calls. I use it for the repeatable parts and spend my time on the judgement: customer insight, positioning and strategy. Teams still need someone to decide what to say, to whom, and why.

How much do marketing freelancers charge?

It depends on experience, scope and market. My rates are on this page: growth strategy plans from $900, messaging and sales kits from $400, steady content from $600 a month, or $45 an hour for support as you need it.

How can I hire a freelancer?

Start with the outcome you want, then look at samples of the freelancer's work and book a call. Agree the scope, price and timeline in writing before work begins, and consider a small first project to see how you work together. If you'd like to start with me, book a free intro call and I'll reply with a scope and a quote.

Is it cheaper to hire a freelancer?

Often, for specific or changing needs. You pay for the work delivered, with no salary, benefits, tools or recruiting costs. If you have a steady full-time workload every week, an employee can work out cheaper per hour.

How much does it cost to hire a marketer?

A full-time marketer costs their salary plus benefits, tools and the time to recruit and manage them, so the real cost sits above the salary. With a freelancer you pay only for scoped work: from $400 for a sales kit, $600 a month for content, or $45 an hour.
